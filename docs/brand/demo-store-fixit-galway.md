# FixIt Galway: demo store brand guideline

FixIt Galway is a made-up independent phone repair shop in Galway city. This guideline is for its
**public online store** only, and is deliberately different from the Sellify platform brand
(`sellify-platform.md`). The whole look is applied **through the Sellify store editor**: template,
colours, fonts, logo, banner and content. Nothing is hand-coded.

## Personality
Local, quick and straight-talking. Like the friendly person behind the counter who fixes your
screen while you grab a coffee. It is not a tech company: no jargon and no hard sell.

## Logo
![FixIt Galway logo](fixit-galway-logo.svg)

- **Mark:** a phone with a wrench, sitting on a Galway Bay wave, on a navy rounded square.
- **Lockup on the store:** the mark followed by the name in the heading font (the store header
  does this automatically).
- **Clear space:** at least a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- **Don'ts:** never recolour it, never use it on a busy photo, and never stretch it.

## Colours
| Role | Name | Hex | Used for (store editor field) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main colour | Corrib Navy | `#12263A` | Buttons, links (*Main colour*) |
| Accent | Hooker Rust | `#E2622F` | Banner, badges such as "Only 2 left" (*Accent*) |
| Page background | Salthill Sand | `#FBF6EE` | Page background (*Page background*) |
| Cards | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, product tiles (*Cards*) |
| Text | Turf | `#1D1D1F` | Body text (*Text*) |

The navy and rust come from Galway hooker boats: dark hulls and rust-red sails. Button text is
white on navy (contrast 15:1).

## Type
- **Headings:** Poppins, bold and rounded: friendly but confident.
- **Body:** Nunito, soft and very readable on phones.
- **Store editor:** heading font *Poppins*, body font *Nunito*.

## Shape and layout
- **Template:** *Local*. The map, opening hours and reviews come first, because most customers
  walk in.
- **Corners:** *Very rounded*. **Buttons:** *Medium*. **Top of homepage:** *Text and photo side by side*.
- **Products per row:** 3.

## Tone of voice
- Short, warm and plain. Write "We'll have your screen done in an hour", not "Our technicians
  will facilitate…".
- Be honest about prices and timing. The price you see is the price you pay.
- Buttons are actions: "Book a repair", "Get an offer", "Add to basket".
- Banner messages are short and specific: "10% off screen repairs this week".
